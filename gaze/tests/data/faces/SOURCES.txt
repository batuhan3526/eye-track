Face images for the deep gaze model tests (tests/test_deep_onnx.py) and for
tools/derin_onnx_cevir.py. All three are PUBLIC DOMAIN: official NASA crew
portraits (works of the U.S. federal government, Wikimedia Commons license
"Public domain", author NASA). Fetched 2026-10-20 as Wikimedia's 960 px
thumbnails, then cropped around the face to 4:3 and resized to 640x480
(webcam-like framing), re-encoded as JPEG quality 88.

reference.json: MediaPipe FaceLandmarker landmarks of each image (468 points,
normalised, rounded to 6 decimals) and the torch + ptgaze CPU gaze output
(DeepGaze(device="cpu").estimate_from_landmarks) for exactly those landmarks.
It lets the ONNX path be checked against torch on machines without torch
(the Linux Docker suite). Regenerate with:
  CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES="" python tools/derin_onnx_cevir.py --referans
